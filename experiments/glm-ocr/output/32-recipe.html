Ingredients
• 2 lbs beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
• 3 tablespoons olive oil
• 1 large onion, diced
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 3 large carrots, cut into 1-inch pieces
• 4 medium potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
• 8 oz mushrooms, halved
• 2 cups beef broth
• 1 cup red wine
• 2 tablespoons tomato paste
• 2 bay leaves
• 1 teaspoon dried thyme
• Salt and black pepper to taste

Chef's Tips

For the best results, use beef chuck which has the right amount of marbling for a tender, flavorful stew. Make this dish a day ahead and reheat—the flavors deepen overnight. You can also slow-cook this in a slow cooker on low for 6-8 hours instead of braising in the oven.
